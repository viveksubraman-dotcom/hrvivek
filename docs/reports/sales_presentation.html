Knowledge Grounding • 06:30 - 08:00

The Tri-Hybrid Search Brain: Eliminating Policy Hallucination

Standard RAG fails on numerical constraints and kinship rules. Our Tri-Hybrid Brain fuses 3 parallel streams into verified policy truth.

The RAG Approximation Trap
Why Standard Vector Search Fails:

Vector search operates on probabilistic cosine similarity. If an employee asks: "My cousin passed away, can I take 5 days?", vector search matches "bereavement" and incorrectly quotes the 5-day immediate family grant. The company pays for unauthorized leave.

3-Stream Convergence via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (k=60)
Stream 1: Dense Vector (text-embedding-004)
Captures semantic conversational intent (cos sim ≥ 0.78, Weight: 0.50)
Weight 0.50
Stream 2: Sparse BM25 Lexical Keyword Match
Pins exact section numbers, clauses & legal acronyms (Weight: 0.30)
Weight 0.30
Stream 3: OKF Semantic Ontology Graph
Evaluates numerical thresholds & kinship gates (Weight: 0.20)
Weight 0.20
RRF(d) = ∑ [w_m / (60 + r_m(d))] across all 3 streams
