The Enterprise Dilemma: Why GenAI Pilots Fail in Corporate Operations

Organizations rushed into generative AI chatbots in 2024–2025, but 85% failed to deliver operational ROI due to three fatal architectural flaws.

⚠️
1. The Hallucination Liability

Standard RAG chatbots invent non-existent policies (e.g. bereavement leave for pets, unauthorized tuition subsidies) or misquote notice periods, exposing HR to statutory legal disputes and union grievances.

Risk: Statutory Labor Non-Compliance
🚫
2. The "Read-Only" Trap

Current chatbots can only regurgitate text summaries. When an employee asks to submit a PTO request or order an approved ergonomic monitor, the bot directs them back to manual Jira/Workday forms.

Impact: Zero L1 Service Desk Deflection

3. The Data Leakage & IDOR Vector

Connecting LLMs directly to APIs invites prompt injections, cross-user IDOR impersonation (querying another employee's salary or home address), and accidental leakage of confidential medical diagnosis (PHI).

Risk: GDPR, HIPAA & PDPA Regulatory Fines

Enterprises do not need another conversation toy. They need a deterministic, zero-trust autonomous execution engine that reasons naturally with LLMs but executes transactions with banking-grade verification.
