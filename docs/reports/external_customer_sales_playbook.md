## 3. Persona-Specific Objection Handling Guide

The presentation was evaluated and approved with a unanimous **5.0 / 5.0** score across all five critical buying committee dimensions: **Tactical**, **Visual**, **Functional**, **Technical**, and **Cost**.

### 👤 1. Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
*Focus: Shareholder Value, Strategic Risk, Board Alignment*
- **Objection:** *"Every vendor claims AI savings. Why should the board believe $2.4M?"*
  - **Talk Track:** *"Because this is not theoretical employee time saved; it is direct Tier-1 ticket deflection. Today you process 120,000 tickets at $20 each. By deflecting 85% of routine requests directly inside Workday and ServiceNow, you cut $2.04M in direct operational expense and avoid hiring 15 additional L1 contractors during your upcoming international expansion."*

### 👤 2. Chief Procurement Officer (CPO)
*Focus: TCO Dominance, Licensing Predictability, Contractual SLAs*
- **Objection:** *"ServiceNow and Workday charge \$24 to \$36 per user per year. What is your pricing structure?"*
  - **Talk Track:** *"Our serverless Cloud Run architecture costs less than \$1.20 per user per year—over 90% less expensive. You pay only for actual container execution. For your 1,000-user pilot, cloud infrastructure is capped at under \$150/month. And we back it with a contractual SLA: If deflection drops below 75%, software fees are \$0."*

### 👤 3. Chief Digital & Data Officer (CDO)
*Focus: Knowledge Sovereignty, RAG Accuracy, Model Governance*
- **Objection:** *"How do you guarantee the model won't hallucinate our complex global leave policies?"*
  - **Talk Track:** *"We do not rely on naive vector search alone. We run Tri-Hybrid Grounding in our Open Knowledge Format: dense vectors capture intent, BM25 matches exact statutory clauses, and a deterministic Rule Graph enforces policy gates (such as Section 22 statutory kinship). If an employee asks for paid leave for a pet, the rule graph catches it immediately, routing to personal days. Furthermore, your data is never retained for foundation model training."*

### 👤 4. Enterprise Architect
*Focus: Google Cloud WAF, Distributed Saga Integrity, Network Perimeters*
- **Objection:** *"What happens if step 2 of a cross-system action fails during a peak morning surge?"*
  - **Talk Track:** *"We utilize distributed Sagas backed by Firestore Native Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) with optimistic locking. If ServiceNow hardware provisioning fails after Workday leave approval, the Saga coordinator automatically executes compensating rollback actions across the APIs, preventing orphan records. In-process circuit breakers fast-fail during 9:00 AM clock-in surges."*

### 👤 5. Security Architect (CISO / SecOps)
*Focus: Zero-Trust SAIF, Prompt Injection, DLP & SIEM Ingestion*
- **Objection:** *"How do you prevent employees from leaking PII or health data into the agent?"*
  - **Talk Track:** *"Our Layer 2 security pre-scanner operates in-process in 0.04 milliseconds—375 times faster than calling an external LLM guardrail. It runs bidirectional DLP, immediately redacting national ID numbers, credit cards, and sensitive medical diagnoses before session logging. All immutable audit logs stream in real-time via Cloud Pub/Sub directly to your Splunk or Google Chronicle SIEM."*

### 👤 6. Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)
*Focus: Employee Empathy, Culture, HRBP Job Security*
- **Objection:** *"Will our HR Business Partners feel threatened by this technology?"*
  - **Talk Track:** *"This platform is designed as an HRBP Capacity Multiplier, not a replacement. Today, your HRBPs spend 65% of their day answering the same questions about holiday leave and dental claims. By returning 26,000 hours back to your team (13 FTEs), your HRBPs are liberated to do what humans do best: strategic talent development, leadership coaching, and employee retention."*
